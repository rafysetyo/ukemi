Assurance crowdfunding on NEAR · Win-win by design

When a campaign misses its target, backers fall softly and come out ahead.

Named after Ukemi (the Japanese art of falling safely), creators commit a Gold Cushion bonus pool upfront. If the target is met, the creator builds and the cushion returns to them. If the campaign falls short, every backer receives 100% of their principal back plus a proportional share of the Gold Cushion.

Step 1 · Creator

Sets a funding target and commits a Gold Cushion up front.

Step 2 · Backers

Pledge NEAR before the deadline. Nothing else is needed.

Step 3 · Deadline

Target met: creator withdraws, cushion returns. Target missed: backers claim principal plus a share of the cushion.

The only rule: the outcome depends on raised versus target. Your bonus = your pledge ÷ total raised × cushion.

Try the math: slide the pledge below to see how your payout changes with the funding result. Open Explore Campaigns for live campaigns on ukemi.testnet, and connect a testnet wallet to back, finalize, claim and withdraw.
